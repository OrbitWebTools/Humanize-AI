# Humanize-AI — Free AI Humanizer & AI Detector (Open-Source, No Login)

A fast, 100% browser-based **2-in-1 AI Humanizer and Free AI Detector** built by **OrbitWebTools**. Designed to help students, writers, and researchers inspect AI patterns and refine ChatGPT, Gemini AI, and Claude AI drafts into natural human prose—with unlimited word counts, APA citation protection, and zero server uploads.

🔗 **Live Web App:** [https://orbitwebtools.github.io/Humanize-AI/](https://orbitwebtools.github.io/Humanize-AI/)

---

## Key Features

* **Built-In Free AI Detector:** Measures AI pattern density, sentence-length burstiness variance, and highlights 150+ common LLM clichés right in your browser.
* **4 Rewriting Modes:**
  * **Academic (Essay):** Formal register, no slang or contractions, tailored for research papers and essays.
  * **Natural:** Smooth, conversational tone for blogs, emails, and articles.
  * **Paraphrase:** Fluency-focused restructuring to eliminate repetitive phrasing.
  * **Simple:** Concise, plain-English clarity.
* **APA Citation & Direct Quote Shield:** Preserves `(Author, 2025)`, `[1]`, and quoted passages untouched during rewriting.
* **Unlimited Word Count & Google Docs Ready:** Live word count, character count, reading time, and 1-click clean text copy for Google Docs and Microsoft Word.
* **100% Client-Side Privacy:** Pure HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. No backend servers, no API keys, no login walls, and zero data logging.
